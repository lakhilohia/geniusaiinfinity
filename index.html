Welcome

A holistic, offline-first companion combining classical Ayurveda (Charaka Samhita, Ashtanga Hridaya, Atharvaveda), modern nutrition and conventional (MBBS) references — for lung, eye, ear, gallbladder, skin, digestive and sleep concerns. Works with no internet connection.

🌐 Language: UI is translated into Hindi, Hinglish, Assamese, Rajasthani, Urdu & Sanskrit. Translations are best-effort; detailed clinical text stays in English for safety.

Educational use only — not medical advice. This app does not diagnose, treat, or cure any disease. Dietary, herbal, acupressure and traditional/spiritual suggestions are general educational and cultural information and can interact with medicines or conditions. Always consult a qualified physician / registered Ayurvedic practitioner before starting any herb, diet or therapy — especially in pregnancy, or with diabetes, hypertension, kidney/liver disease, or gallstones needing surgical care.

🚑 Emergency signs — turant hospital jaayein

In me se koi bhi ho to diet/herb par time mat gawaayein — seedha doctor / hospital:

  • Saans: bahut tez saans phoolna, honth/naakhun neele padna, seene me dard.
  • Pet (gallbladder): tej pet dard + bukhaar + aankh/peshaab peela (jaundice) — emergency.
  • Aankh: achanak dikhna kam/band, tej dard, chot lagna.
  • Kaan: tej dard + pus/khoon nikalna + bukhaar.
  • General: behoshi, bahut kamzori, seene/haath me dard, lakwa jaise lakshan.

India emergency number: 112 · Ambulance: 108
